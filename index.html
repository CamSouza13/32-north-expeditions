Partners

Partner standards

32 North runs its local trips and books international expeditions that other operators run. An operator has to pass every check below before we list it or send it a guest.

1

Licence and permits

The operator holds the licences and land or water permits its trips require where it runs them.

2

Insurance

We keep a current certificate of the operator's liability insurance on file.

3

Guide qualifications

Each lead guide holds a recognised qualification for the activity and current wilderness first aid training.

4

Emergency plan

The operator has a written emergency and evacuation plan for each trip, and we read it.

5

Group size

The operator states its guide-to-guest ratio for each trip, and we publish it.

6

References

We speak with past clients or other companies that have worked with the operator.

The checklist follows two published references: ISO 21101, the international standard for adventure tourism safety management, and the Adventure Travel Trade Association's guide qualification and performance standard.

Questions

Common questions

Ask anything else by email: cameron@orbitalecology.com

When do trips start?

We are scheduling the first dates now. People who request dates receive them before we publish.

What does a trip cost?

Each trip is priced separately. We send the price with the dates. Requesting dates is free.

How do I pay?

This site takes no payment. We send payment details with your confirmed dates.

Who runs the trips?

32 North runs the local trips. Partner operators run the international expeditions, and we name the operator before you book. See partner standards.

How do you choose partners?

Each operator has to pass six checks covering licences, insurance, guide qualifications, emergency planning, group size and references. See partner standards.

Do I need experience?

Tell us what you have done and we will recommend a trip. Each expedition has its own requirements, and we send them with the dates.

What about cancellation?

Cancellation terms depend on the operator. We send them in writing with your dates, before you pay.

Where do trips leave from?

Local trips leave from San Diego. International expeditions start at the destination, and we help you plan the travel.
